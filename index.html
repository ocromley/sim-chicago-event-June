This invitation-only gathering is intended for senior women CIOs, their direct reports, and other senior women technology leaders. This invitation is non-transferable. Due to limited capacity, cancellations or no-shows cannot be accommodated.

Featured Panelists

Accomplished industry and executive search leaders sharing practical perspectives on navigating the path to the boardroom

Art Hopkins

Global Head of NextGen Tech Officers Practice, Russell Reynolds Associates

Art Hopkins brings deep expertise in executive search and leadership advisory, specializing in technology officer placements and board readiness for senior technology leaders across global enterprises.

Dorri McWhorter

President & CEO, The Executives' Club of Chicago

Dorri McWhorter is a recognized leader in executive development and corporate governance, bringing extensive experience in building leadership pipelines and fostering executive excellence in the Chicago business community.

Sylvia Prickel

Chicago Chapter Executive Committee Chair, Sponsor Co-Chair & Previous Board Chair, Private Directors Association

Sylvia Prickel offers firsthand perspective on board service and private directorship, with a track record of guiding organizations through governance challenges and building effective board cultures.

Moderator

Ann Madea

Retired EVP & Chief Information Officer, Simmons Bank

Ann Madea brings decades of experience as a senior technology executive who has worked closely with corporate boards. Her perspective bridges the gap between technology leadership and governance, making her the ideal moderator for this important conversation.

Discussion Topics

Practical perspectives on navigating the path to the boardroom and contributing effectively once you are there

  • What boards look for in prospective directors
  • Positioning yourself for future board opportunities
  • How the board recruitment and selection process actually works
  • Building the experience, network, and visibility that make you board-ready
  • Developing effective relationships with your own company's board
  • The value technology executives bring to corporate governance
  • Lessons learned and practical advice from experienced board leaders

"Whether you are regularly engaging with your organization's board today, actively pursuing board service, or preparing for opportunities later in your career, this discussion will offer practical insight and valuable executive perspective."

About SIM Chicago

Society for Information Management

SIM is the premier network for IT leaders, comprised of over 5,000 members across 40 chapters in the United States. For over five decades, SIM has been the leading organization for senior IT executives, providing a unique forum for networking, professional development, and thought leadership.

Our Mission:

  • To nurture a community of senior IT executives who support each other in their professional growth
  • To foster meaningful connections between IT leaders across industries
  • To advance the IT profession through knowledge sharing and mentorship
  • To promote diversity and inclusion in technology leadership

SIM Chicago Chapter:

The Chicago chapter is one of SIM's most active communities, hosting regular events that bring together CIOs, CTOs, and senior technology leaders from the region's top organizations. Our Executive Women in Tech series is dedicated to empowering women in technology leadership roles.

Featured Speaker

Lisa Davis

Fortune 500 Executive & Bestselling Author

Lisa Davis is a visionary force in digital transformation and leadership in the AI era, with over 25 years of experience driving innovation across government, academia, high tech, and healthcare.

A former Fortune 100 tech executive who has led large-scale modernization efforts across global enterprises, she has built a career turning technology into tangible business growth while redefining inclusive leadership, not as a cultural play, but as a competitive advantage that accelerates growth and fuels business success.

Now, in her next chapter, Lisa is focused on shaping the next generation of leaders in the age of AI. She brings a uniquely holistic approach to transformation, understanding that progress isn't just powered by technology, it's enabled by people, culture, and process.

As an advisor, board member, and author of #1 Amazon bestselling book The Only Woman in the Room, she is a fierce advocate for elevating women and diverse talent in STEM and for rebuilding the systems that have kept them out.

In sharing her own experiences and working alongside leaders and organizations ready to meet the moment, Lisa is helping to shape a future where equity and excellence power one another. She's not celebrating having been the only woman in the room, she's building a future where no one else has to be. The Only Woman in the Room: How to Win in a Workplace (Still!) Built for Men now available on Amazon.

Her journey from early career challenges to executive leadership provides invaluable insights for women navigating the tech industry. Lisa's authentic storytelling and practical wisdom make her one of the most sought-after speakers on leadership and diversity in technology.

Bestselling Book

Lisa is the author of The Only Woman in the Room: How to Thrive in Workplaces Built for MenIt is a candid, empowering guide that draws from her personal experiences to help women succeed in male-dominated industries. The book has become essential reading for women in tech and business leadership.

It invites readers into the real story of what it takes to lead as a woman when the rules weren't written with you in mind, but you're determined to win anyway.

She's not celebrating having been the only woman in the room, she's building a future where no one else has to be.
